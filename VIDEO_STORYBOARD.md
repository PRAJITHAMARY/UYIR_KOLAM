# UYIR KOLAM — CONCEPT VIDEO STORYBOARD
### Duration: 75 Seconds | Challenge: Everyday Magic (DJXHack Residency 2026)

---

## Storyboard Overview & Production Notes

- **Tone**: Meditative, warm, poetic, and intimate. Begins with the quiet dawn of South India and unfolds into generative digital wonder.
- **Audio Ambience**: Begins with faint dawn ambient sounds (distant temple bells, early morning birdsong), transitioning into procedural harmonized chimes (*Raag Mohanam*) synced with visual actions.
- **Visual Style**: Clean editorial macro videography, warm sunlight on stone thresholds, transitioning into screen recordings of the functioning prototype.

---

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Dawn Threshold (0:00 – 0:12)
- **Timing**: 12 seconds
- **Visuals**:
  - Close-up shot of an earthen doorstep being washed with clean water in the soft blue-gold light of dawn.
  - A woman's fingers dip into a bowl of coarsely ground white rice flour (*arisi maavu*).
  - She begins dropping white dots (*pulli*) on the ground with effortless precision.
  - An ant pauses near the flour, nibbling on a grain.
- **Narration (Voiceover - Calm, Warm)**:
  *“Every morning across South India, before the sun rises, an ancient conversation begins at the doorstep. White rice flour, drawn on stone. An offering of beauty, and a daily breakfast for the tiniest creatures of the earth.”*
- **Audio**: Distant dawn birdsong, soft rustle of rice powder.

---

### Scene 2: The Question of Everyday Magic (0:12 – 0:22)
- **Timing**: 10 seconds
- **Visuals**:
  - Camera glides from the traditional doorstep into an illuminated creative workspace.
  - On the screen, the **Uyir Kolam** welcome interface glows warmly in tones of ivory, terracotta, and marigold.
  - The tagline appears: *“Every line brings a little life.”*
- **Narration**:
  *“What if this sacred geometry didn’t just symbolically feed life—what if human intention could make it breathe?”*
- **Audio**: Soft resonant temple chime (*tingsha* bell) rings cleanly.

---

### Scene 3: The Living Gesture (0:22 – 0:42)
- **Timing**: 20 seconds
- **Visuals**:
  - *Direct screen capture of the functioning prototype.*
  - A finger touches the tablet canvas. A luminous firefly awakens, pulsing with golden light.
  - As the finger traces a smooth curve around the pulli dots, tender green vines and leaves sprout dynamically along the stroke.
  - The user crosses two lines: instantly, a pink sacred Lotus blossoms outward with an elastic bounce.
  - The user toggles **8-Fold Symmetry**: with one continuous spiral gesture, an entire radiant mandala bursts into full bloom across the canvas.
- **Narration**:
  *“Meet Uyir Kolam. In this digital sanctuary, strokes become nutrient-bearing vines. Every curve sprouts living tendrils. Every line crossing blossoms into sacred lotus and jasmine. And every touch awakens drifting fireflies.”*
- **Audio**: Real procedural audio from the Web Audio engine—delicate pentatonic harp notes that pitch with stroke speed, and crystalline chimes upon flower bloom.

---

### Scene 4: The Artwork Reveal & Meditative Joy (0:42 – 0:58)
- **Timing**: 16 seconds
- **Visuals**:
  - User taps **“Reveal Garden”**.
  - The canvas eases into celebration mode: delicate flower petals drift across the screen like a morning breeze.
  - Garden statistics gently emerge: *“12 Sacred Blooms Awakened • 38 Living Vines • 42 Fireflies Dancing”*.
  - The message appears: *“A little magic, made by you.”*
  - The user clicks **“Save Artwork”**, downloading a crisp, high-resolution PNG keepsake.
- **Narration**:
  *“There are no game scores or timers here. Only a quiet, rewarding celebration of what your hands brought into being. An ancient ritual of mindfulness, made tangible for our digital age.”*
- **Audio**: Full dawn harmony chord (*Raag Mohanam* scale) fading gently into peace.

---

### Scene 5: The Horizon (0:58 – 1:15)
- **Timing**: 17 seconds
- **Visuals**:
  - Conceptual visualization: A lightweight projector mounted above a real doorway, projecting the digital garden directly onto the physical doorstep floor as someone draws with real rice flour.
  - Fade to the title card:
    - **UYIR KOLAM** (உயிர் கோலம்)
    - *Every Line Brings a Little Life*
    - DJXHack Residency 2026 • Everyday Magic
- **Narration**:
  *“Today, a zero-install interactive canvas. Tomorrow, a bridge between real doorstep sand and living light. Uyir Kolam—rediscovering everyday magic, one line at a time.”*
- **Audio**: Final resonant bell chime, fading into morning stillness.

## Feature & Asset Transparency

- **Functioning Prototype Features Shown**:
  - Interactive HTML5 canvas drawing engine with DPI scaling and midpoint curve smoothing.
  - Real-time botanical life generator (vines on curvature, flowers on intersections, fireflies on endpoints).
  - Authentic Pulli dot grids (5×5, 7×7) with proximity snapping.
  - 4-fold and 8-fold sacred symmetry modes.
  - Procedural Web Audio API sound synthesis.
  - High-resolution PNG artwork export.
